## Team
The work this website is based upon comes from research contributions from the greater Trichopterology community. Compiling species names, literature, and records was largely the work of John C. Morse over the past several decades through the Trichoptera World Checklist.

As we continue to add, update, and compile additional data in furthering this endeavor, we encourage you to _please contact us if you would like to contribute to this effort._

## History
The Trichoptera World Checklist (TWC) was first published in 1990 and has been available on the web for over ## years. Dr. John C. Morse was the founding editor and original data were pulled from the Trichopterorum Catalogus (Fischer 1960-1973), the Zoological Record (1961-2008, and compiled literature from trichopterologists. In 1999, the database was transferred to a MySQL database hosted by Clemson University. Recently, the TWC has been transitioned from SQL formatting into a TaxonWorks project.

## Data

Data for these pages is collaboratively curated online in a [TaxonWorks](https://taxonworks) project. All data behind this site served as JSON through calls to a [TaxonWorks API](https://api.taxonworks.org). You can follow along by using the Console of your browser to explore the Network tab.
